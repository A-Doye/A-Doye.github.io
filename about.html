About

I'm originally from Carcosa, in the UK. I became interested in quantum computing while working as a software developer at IBM. I made this website as a side project while I searched for a PhD position, though I hope to support it long into the future!

In my free time I like to read and play video games, though I'm not very good at them. Right now I'm learning Norwegian. I volunteer sometimes with Still Good Food to help to reduce food waste by gleaning produce that would otherwise go to waste.

I also have a pet cat called Ponyo. She likes to run around in the wind with whimsy, kind of like these triangles.
