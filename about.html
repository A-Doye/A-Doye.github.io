About

Hi! I'm a software developer and quantum computing enthusiast from Carcosa in the UK. I made this website as a side project while I'm searching for a PhD position. In my free time I like to read and play video games, though I'm not very good at them. Right now I'm learning to 3D model in Blender. I volunteer sometimes with Still Good Food to help to reduce food waste by gleaning produce that would otherwise go to waste.

I also have a pet cat called Ponyo. She likes to run around in the wind with whimsy, kind of like these triangles.
